## 1. The Core Purpose: Why Kodedock Exists

Modern software engineering moves at breakneck speed, yet developers spend **60% of their engineering cycles reinventing foundational wheels**: authentication, payment webhooks, role-based dashboards, database models, and cloud configs.

At the same time, millions of talented software engineers, freelancers, and designers build high-quality codebases and boilerplates, but have **zero dedicated, developer-friendly platforms** to sell their work in India. 

Existing platforms force Indian creators to deal with foreign payment processors (PayPal/Stripe), lose 5% to 8% in foreign exchange (Forex) fees, wait weeks for payouts, or surrender **35% to 50% commission** to legacy platforms like Envato.

## 2. The Problems We Are Solving

### 2.1 The Buyer Problem (Devs, Startups, Indie Hackers)
1. **The Time Sink**: Starting a SaaS or client project takes 3 to 6 weeks just to get auth, payments, database schemas, and layouts functional before writing a single line of business logic.
2. **Foreign Card & Dollar Barrier**: Most international templates are priced in USD ($49 - $199). Indian developers frequently experience credit card transaction failures or lack international credit cards entirely.
3. **Legacy Bloat & Technical Debt**: Existing marketplaces (Envato / ThemeForest) are trapped in the 2012 era of jQuery, PHP, and bloated WordPress templates that modern developers do not use.
4. **Zero Upgrade Path**: When an author updates a template on Gumroad or Substack, buyers receive messy email attachments with broken patch notes and zero version control.

### 2.2 The Seller Problem (Tech Creators & Senior Engineers)
1. **Predatory Marketplace Fees**: Legacy platforms like Envato charge non-exclusive authors **up to 45%–55% commission** plus withholding taxes and buyer fees.
2. **High Forex & Payout Friction**: Selling in USD means losing 4%–6% to PayPal/Stripe currency conversion, plus cross-border transfer delays.
3. **Generic Platforms Lack Code Context**: Gumroad and Lemon Squeezy are built for PDFs, ebooks, and courses. They do not understand Git, tech-stack tagging, live preview sandboxes, or version releases (`v1.0.0` -> `v1.1.0`).
4. **Piracy & Code Theft**: Selling code as raw zip links leads to instant piracy, re-uploading, and link sharing with zero cryptographic license validation.
